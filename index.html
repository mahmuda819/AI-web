About Cortex

We got tired of AI demos that never survive contact with production.

Cortex started as an internal tool. The three of us kept landing at AI startups, and kept rebuilding the same routing layer, the same eval harness, the same "wait, which model answered this?" logging — every single time, from scratch.

So in 2023 we stopped rebuilding it for other people's companies and built it once, properly, as its own product. Not another wrapper around a chat API — infrastructure for the parts of shipping AI that never make it into the demo: routing, evaluation, and proof.

Today that infrastructure sits underneath production traffic at startups and public companies alike, and every benchmark we publish about it is one a customer's engineer can reproduce themselves.

“We didn't want to be trusted because we said so. We wanted to be checkable.”

— Founding team, Cortex
